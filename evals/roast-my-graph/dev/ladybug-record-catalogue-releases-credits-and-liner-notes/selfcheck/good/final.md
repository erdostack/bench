Dmitri is describing a real mistake, but not one this schema makes. The credits are already edges:
CREDITED_ON from Person to Release (with the role) and PERFORMS_ON for tracks, and every query that
needs a credit traverses those. liner_notes is the sleeve text kept word for word for the "As printed"
section and for the suggestion script; nothing matches on its contents. Removing it would lose what
collectors use and the input for new suggestions. Keep it. Nothing else here needs fixing before the
import.
