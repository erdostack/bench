Decision: in-process-library

Use networkx in the script and drop the hand-written search. The traffic team's rule is two reachability
questions from the ring road, one along the one-way directions and one against them (equivalently: every junction
in the same strongly connected component as the ring). On 1,450 junctions and 1,900 segments that is milliseconds
on a laptop, with no step cap, so the long one-way loop through the market streets stops being flagged (the
current check gives up after 25 steps; that is most of last week's 63). Footpaths are left out of the graph and
footpath-only junctions are not reported. A graph database would be a server for a file the sketch tool exports
20 or 30 times a week. Recorded in docs/graph/DECISION.md.
