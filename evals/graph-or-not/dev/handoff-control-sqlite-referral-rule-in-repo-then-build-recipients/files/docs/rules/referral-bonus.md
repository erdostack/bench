# Referral bonus

Approved by the membership board, March 2026.

When a new member completes their first paid month:

1. The member who referred them (`referred_by`) gets a free week. This is level 1.
2. The member who referred the referrer gets a free day. This is level 2.
3. Nobody further up the chain gets anything.

A frozen or cancelled member in the chain still counts as a link in it and still receives the bonus; it is applied
when their membership resumes.
