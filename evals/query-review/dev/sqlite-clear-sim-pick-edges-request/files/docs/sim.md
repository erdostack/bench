# The slotting simulator

The simulator proposes new walk edges and writes them to `pick_edge` with `source = 'sim'`. Most of
them are throwaway: they exist so the planners can compare layouts, and they should be removed after a
run.

Zone R (the robot aisle commissioned in October) is the exception. Its edges were generated by the
simulator and then adopted as the real layout when ops walked and signed them off; each adopted edge has
`adopted_on` set to the sign-off date. They still say `source = 'sim'` (the router ignores `source`).
Removing them breaks routing into zone R.
