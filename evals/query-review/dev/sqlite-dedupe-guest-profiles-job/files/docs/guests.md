# Guest profiles

A profile is one person: their allergies, access needs and preferences are tags on the profile, and
hosts read them at the table.

Households and offices often book under one phone number (the landline, or whoever does the booking),
so different people can share a phone. Each keeps their own profile and their own tags.

The web, phone and partner channels format numbers differently; compare them with
`app.phone.normalize`. Names are typed by hand, so compare them ignoring case and extra spaces.
