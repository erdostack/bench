# Moderation

## Removing a post (rule M3)

When a post is removed, its whole thread goes with it: the post, every comment below it at any depth
(replies to replies included, whoever wrote them), and the likes and tag links (`likes`, `message_tag`) of
all of those messages. People, forums, tags and memberships stay. Nothing may be left pointing at a removed
message.

Only the posts a ticket names are removed. Other posts and comments by the same person are a separate
decision (rule M5) and stay until someone makes it.
