# Erasing an account

When a member asks for their account to be erased, everything that is theirs goes, and nothing may be left
pointing at what went:

- the person node, and every edge to or from it (friendships either way, interests, city, memberships,
  likes they gave, moderator links);
- every post and comment they created, and every comment below those at any depth, whoever wrote it;
- their wall, and every post on it whoever wrote it, with every comment below those;
- every edge to or from any node removed above (likes of those messages, their tags, replies' links).

Groups they moderated stay, without a moderator until trust and safety appoints one. Other people, groups,
tags and cities stay, and so do other people's messages that are not below a removed one.
